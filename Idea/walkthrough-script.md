# GitHubIQ — Product Walkthrough Voiceover Script

Narrated by the deployed Azure Speech voice (`en-US-AndrewMultilingualNeural`).
Rendered to `Idea/walkthrough.mp4` (1080p) by `build-video.js`.

---

## 1 · The problem  *(visual: problem / solution)*
Every developer knows the feeling. You join a new project and you're staring at thousands of files, outdated READMEs, and a mental model that lives only in a senior engineer's head. Ramp-up is slow, and every question interrupts a teammate.

## 2 · Meet GitHubIQ  *(visual: cover)*
GitHubIQ turns any Git repository into a living, interactive tutor. Point it at a repo, and six specialist A-I agents read the entire codebase and build an onboarding guide that's tailored to you.

## 3 · Point it at a repo  *(visual: hero)*
It starts with a single URL. Paste any public GitHub repository and GitHubIQ gets to work — no setup, and nothing leaves your workspace.

## 4 · Tailor your guide  *(visual: questionnaire)*
Tell it who you are — your role, your experience, and what you want to learn. You can even add custom instructions to steer the analysis toward the parts of the codebase you care about most.

## 5 · Six agents, in parallel  *(visual: analysis)*
Then the crew goes to work. A researcher maps the entry points and hands each specialist its reading list, while the architect, schema, data-flow, tutor and presenter agents run in parallel — every one an expert in its own domain.

## 6 · Your guided onboarding  *(visual: guide)*
Minutes later you get a guide — not a wall of text. It opens with a plain-language overview: what the project is, what it does, how it works, the key files to read first, and a clear, component-by-component breakdown.

## 7 · Live architecture map  *(visual: architecture)*
Explore the architecture as a living map. Drag the components like a whiteboard, zoom and pan the canvas, go full screen, and tap any node to see its technology and exactly how it connects to the rest of the system.

## 8 · Follow a real request  *(visual: data flow)*
Follow a real request end to end. Walk it hop by hop down the left rail and watch the data transform at every step — with the exact files and code behind each move.

## 9 · Reverse-engineered schema  *(visual: schema)*
The database is reverse-engineered into a draggable diagram — tables, columns, and the foreign keys that link them — all explained in plain English.

## 10 · Understand any codebase  *(visual: system architecture)*
That's GitHubIQ. Stop reading stale docs and start understanding. Point it at your next repository, and build a real mental model in minutes.
